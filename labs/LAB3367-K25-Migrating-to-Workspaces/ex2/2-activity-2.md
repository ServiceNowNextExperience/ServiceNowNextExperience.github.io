## Overview
In this activity, you will add a new dashboard page in CSM/FSM Configurable Workspace experiences


## Instructions
1. Navigate to **All -> UI Builder**

2. Click on the **Experiences** tab, search for **CSM/FSM Configurable Workspace**

3. Click on **CSM/FSM Configurable Workspace**

4. Click on Page and variants **+**

5. Click on **create a new page** on the left box

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FZQyCyfwnPGLOkSXNcGSR%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3fe88772-d6e4-402f-8cd1-f0a7febd1ee6)


6. Search for **dashboard** and choose a template as below. Note: click on the **Dashboards** template and not the **Dashboard Overview**

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FKwYFwkgik5s9tpEsdDou%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25207.52.34%25E2%2580%25AFAM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D3d63c580-a22c-41be-a50a-1b178b2d8fe6)

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FUUkN9FoNPO4IhDgf0HPS%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25207.52.55%25E2%2580%25AFAM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3Dc615cb39-00d8-47f6-bc91-07a429f27ad9)
