## Overview
In this activity, you will add an icon to our new pages - K25 CSM Page 1 and K25 CSM Page 2

## Instructions
1. Select **K25 Page 2** in UI Builder and click on **Settings** located at the top banner

2. Click on the blue **Open records** hyperlink and select **Page collection**  
    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FTtqN3VAc80KLzTnkKYuM%252Fimage.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D9bfb9401-70aa-4701-97c4-4f554e83729b)

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FdHonjIWWseSBMACs4ODp%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25205.52.17%25E2%2580%25AFPM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D457a0c88-95f8-4486-bc90-863392022417)
