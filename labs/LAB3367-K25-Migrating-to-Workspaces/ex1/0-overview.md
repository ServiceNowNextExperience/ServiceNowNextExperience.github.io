## Scenario
Bradley Stilton is one of your favorite customer service agents but unfortunately is not very good at navigating ServiceNow. You, as a system admin, decide to add a shortcut for him.

Objective: Create a new category called "My top priority" to display at the top of the Workspace List and add second list named "Critical cases" which are in Open status and Critical priority and group by assignment group for so Benjamin can work on the most important cases.
