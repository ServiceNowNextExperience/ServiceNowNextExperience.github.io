## Overview
In this activity, you will update the UX Lists UX List Categories and  for the CSM/FSM Configurable Workspace

## Instructions
1. In the CSM/FSM list menu configuration, you can view the total categories and prebuilt lists. Click on ‘UX Lists’ tab and click on New

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FHHhymochKVAhRDvVevI0%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25208.20.56%25E2%2580%25AFAM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D6faa9577-79a5-4c89-8c30-36c569955e1a)


2. Create a new list with conditions to display critical cases and facilitate the process of an agent assigning the case to themself

    - Provide the details below and click **Submit**

      - Title = Critical Cases
      - Category = My Top Priority
      - Order = 10
      - Table = Case (sn_customerservice_case)
      - Conditions:
      - State is Open AND Priority is 1-Critical
      - View = CSM Workspace


   - In the List Feature Flags section, select all of the above:

      - Click the box next to "Show checkboxes on hover"
      - Click the box next to "Hide column grouping"
      - Click the box next to "Hide reference links"
      - Click the box next to "Hide drag and drop"
      - Click the box next to "Hide pagination"
          

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252F2nWKdvBilNtRIYKdvWvi%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25208.26.44%25E2%2580%25AFAM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D5ebbb322-e102-4e34-add2-af1fcaa58fb9)


3. Now navigate back to the CSM/FSM Workspace and check out your changes. Confirm the new list displays as expected. Pro tip: you might need to click the refresh button on your browser tab to see the updates

    ![](https://www.gitbook.com/cdn-cgi/image/dpr=2,width=760,onerror=redirect,format=auto/https%3A%2F%2Ffiles.gitbook.com%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FvjXMCrVxKM4rIxrP7AcN%252Fuploads%252FL3uCUATnFHYGDRHIfud6%252FScreenshot%25202025-04-21%2520at%25208.27.28%25E2%2580%25AFAM.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D047177ca-e806-4bc0-93a5-dfb454f86a90)
